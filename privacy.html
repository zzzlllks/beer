Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Act as a beer, a fictional comedy browser game created as an independent developer project, handles information in the current version.

Current version summary: the game does not collect personal information, does not provide accounts, does not process payments, and stores gameplay data locally in your browser using localStorage.

1. Information We Collect

The current version of the game does not intentionally collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. There is no account system, login system, payment system, analytics SDK, real advertising SDK, or backend database in the current version.

2. Local Browser Storage

The game uses browser localStorage to save gameplay data on your device. This may include:

  • Total points and high scores.
  • Win, failure, break, and expiration counts.
  • Customer purchase statistics.
  • Unlocked or configured outfit/decorative item data.
  • Game settings and progress-related values.

This data is stored locally in your browser. The developer does not receive this localStorage data from the current version. You can delete it by clearing your browser site data/localStorage or by using an in-game reset option if available.

3. Device Motion Controls

The game may request access to device orientation or motion data so that you can tilt your phone to control gameplay. In the current version, this data is used only during gameplay in your browser and is not sent to the developer. You can refuse or disable motion permission through your browser or device settings.

4. Mock Advertising

The current version uses mock rewarded-ad buttons. These buttons simulate an ad delay and then grant an in-game reward. No real advertising SDK is connected in the current version, and the mock ad buttons do not send personal information to an ad provider.

5. Future Advertising

Future versions may add real third-party advertising or rewarded ads. If real ads are added, third-party ad providers may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies, such as device identifiers, approximate location, interaction data, or ad performance data. This Privacy Policy will be updated before or when those services are introduced.

6. Hosting Providers and Technical Logs

If you access the game through a hosted website, the hosting provider, browser, network provider, or platform may process technical information such as IP address, request URLs, user agent, timestamps, and error logs. This is controlled by those third parties and their own policies, not by the game's local-only code.

7. Children's Privacy

The game is a fictional comedy game and is not intended to encourage alcohol consumption. The current version does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a future version adds features that collect personal information, additional safeguards and policy updates may be required.

8. Data Sharing

In the current version, the developer does not sell, rent, or share personal information because the game does not intentionally collect personal information. Local gameplay data remains in your browser unless you or your device/browser tools export, sync, share, or delete it.

9. Data Security

Because current gameplay data is stored in browser localStorage, it depends on your device, browser, and account security. localStorage is not designed for highly sensitive information, and the game does not ask you to store sensitive personal information in it.

10. Your Choices

You can:

  • Clear local game data through your browser settings.
  • Use the game's reset option if available.
  • Decline device motion permissions.
  • Stop using the game at any time.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the game changes, especially if accounts, analytics, real advertising, payments, online services, or new platforms are added. The effective date above will be changed when updates are published.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the developer through the project repository: https://github.com/zzzlllks/beer.
